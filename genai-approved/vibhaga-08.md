Answer: आम् / एव
Status: Confirmed
Source-Section: विभागः 8

Note: The blanks don't correspond to any noun in the word bank; parallels Q111 ("______, वयम् अजाः स्मः।" → आम्). आम् (yes) opens the sentence, एव (indeed) emphasizes गायकाः → "आम्, वयम् एव गायकाः स्मः।" The pair is written "आम् / एव" (slash, not comma) so it parses as a single MCQ option.
